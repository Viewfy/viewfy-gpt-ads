A few things worth knowing before you pick:

AMS integration is the real dividing line. The generic tools ($30–$200/month) will answer calls and take messages fine, but someone on your team still has to re-key everything into EZLynx or HawkSoft. Sonant's pitch is that everything gets transcribed and pushed into the management system so staff can act on it immediately, and its pricing bundles setup, AMS integrations, training, and support with no separate charges. That's what you're paying the premium for. Note that Sonant doesn't publish pricing, so the ~$299 figure in the card is a third-party estimate, not official.
