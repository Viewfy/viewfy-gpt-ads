One important insurance rule

Whichever you choose, I would configure the AI to never interpret coverage, recommend limits, promise that something is covered, bind coverage, or make policy changes requiring licensed judgment. Use it for intake and service routing:

“New quote → collect information → producer.”
“Claim → collect basic details → carrier/CSR.”
“Billing → appropriate carrier/CSR.”
“Policy change → collect request → licensed staff.”
“Certificate/COI → collect request → service team/approved automated workflow.”

That setup gets most of the labor savings without creating unnecessary E&O exposure.

My ranking for a typical small independent agency: Sonant if you want deep AMS automation; Rosie if you want the best value and fast deployment; Smith.ai if human backup is your highest priority.
