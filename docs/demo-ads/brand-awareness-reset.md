The original raster images are creative artwork. The separate official SVG wordmark remains the exact verified brand asset, now more prominent above each card. Product statements come from the collected Superagent profile and inbound/outbound product pages.

## Direction for future ads

Keep the Superagent name, logo, and insurance-agency category prominent. Use one direct product benefit per ad. Ground FOMO in the opportunity cost of missed calls and delayed follow-up. Keep concrete capabilities tied to the collected evidence; avoid fabricated scarcity, adoption claims, performance figures, invented customer examples, and decorative metaphors that distract from brand recognition.

The earlier generated imagery and six variants are retained as superseded work. Their catalog is archived and hidden from the active chooser. They are not selected in the current demo. The user's research, brief, campaign, and tracking settings are unchanged.
