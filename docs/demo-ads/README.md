# Superagent demo ads

Two creative directions approved on September 8, 2026. The generated images are standalone artwork; the local demo supplies the original Superagent wordmark and editable headline, body, and CTA. These are illustrative creative concepts, not captured customer outcomes or published campaigns.

## Your old leads have unfinished business.

**Body:** Bring dormant insurance leads back into the conversation with AI follow-up.

**CTA:** See Superagent in action

**Destination:** https://getsuperagent.com/outbound-ai-agent

**Artwork:** A fractured ice block holds dormant insurance lead cards. An amber pulse draws one card into a sequence of phone, message, email, and appointment symbols. Cinematic charcoal, cream, and amber.

**Research:** The saved competitive insights identify missed-call messaging in both Smith.ai and Superagent creatives. This concept demonstrates a different part of Superagent's product: working existing lead lists across calls, texts, and email. The [outbound product page](https://getsuperagent.com/outbound-ai-agent) describes list activation and qualified producer handoffs; the [follow-up guide](https://news.getsuperagent.com/work-every-insurance-lead-without-hiring) describes coordinated outreach and win-back campaigns. These product descriptions support the workflow, not a guaranteed sales result.

## Don’t make them start over.

**Body:** Superagent qualifies the call and gives your producer the context to pick up the conversation.

**CTA:** See the handoff

**Destination:** https://getsuperagent.com/inbound-ai-agent

**Artwork:** A voice waveform becomes an amber ribbon carrying three cream cards into a human producer's hands. The illustrative details are “Auto quote,” “Two vehicles,” and “Renewal next month.” Warm editorial photography with a simple sculptural composition.

**Research:** Collected buyer discussions ask whether AI handles useful next steps or merely takes messages. Saved competitor research also shows human-service and escalation messaging. Superagent's [inbound product page](https://getsuperagent.com/inbound-ai-agent) describes qualification, calendar booking, and handing over full call context. This creative makes the producer's role and the information transfer visible.
